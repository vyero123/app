The pieces are invisible. Every square is tinted by the influence falling on it — the squares each side currently attacks or defends.

  • Amber — only White controls it. Cyan — only Black.
  • Violet — both sides control it. The violet leans warm when White has more attackers, cool when Black does.
  • The more pieces bearing on a square, the deeper the colour.
  • A square with a piece on it gets a glowing ring — white for White's pieces, black for Black's. Which piece it is stays hidden. Tap one to spotlight just that piece's influence in green.
  • The title is the control. Tap Shadow to turn the influence map on and off, and Chess to show or hide the pieces. Turn both on to see the position with its shadows laid over it. Keyboard: S and P.

Pawns control their two forward diagonals only, never the square they push to. Sliding pieces stop at the first piece they meet — no x-ray. Defending your own piece counts as influence. Full definition & sources.
